Staying debt-free as a student

Build a buffer before you borrow. Aim to keep 1–2 months of essential expenses in savings so surprise costs never need a credit card.

If you must borrow, prioritize subsidized education loans over personal loans or credit cards — the interest cost is dramatically lower.

Watch "buy now, pay later" tools. They feel free upfront but function like short-term debt — track them the same way you'd track a loan.

Compare total repayment, not just the EMI. A lower monthly payment over more years can cost more overall. Use the Loan Calculator below.

Quick tips

Automate a small transfer to savings the day your stipend lands — even ₹200 builds the habit.

Track subscriptions monthly; unused ones are the easiest budget leak to fix.

Avoid carrying a credit card balance — interest rates on unpaid balances are typically 30%+ annually.

Use student discounts on software, transit, and food deliberately; they're a real, guilt-free way to cut spend.
