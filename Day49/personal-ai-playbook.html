What is this?

This is your Personal AI Playbook — a private toolkit for turning the way you already use Claude and ChatGPT into reusable, repeatable systems instead of one-off prompts. It lives entirely in this file: nothing is sent anywhere, everything saves to your browser's local storage.

📚 WorkflowsReady-made, editable prompt templates for your app-building, job search, and data work. Fill in the blanks, copy, paste.
🧩 Prompt BuilderAssemble a brand-new prompt from labelled building blocks (role, objective, constraints…) with a live preview as you go.
🔁 Loop BuilderTurn any prompt into a self-checking, self-correcting "run until done" prompt with clear stop conditions.
⚙️ SettingsImport/export your saved workflows as a JSON file, switch themes, and see keyboard shortcuts.

What is the Personal AI Playbook?

A quick tour of every section.

1
DashboardYour home screen — a quick explanation of the tool, your stats, and shortcuts to your favorite workflows.
2
WorkflowsA library of fillable prompt templates, grouped into App Building, Job Search, and Data Analysis. Search, filter, favorite, duplicate, edit, or write your own.
3
Prompt BuilderBuild a one-off prompt from labelled blocks — role, objective, context, constraints, reasoning strategy, output format, tone, examples, quality checks — with a live preview. Save the result as a new workflow.
4
Loop BuilderWrap a task in an autonomous loop: define the goal, how success is evaluated, what to change when it fails, when to stop, and safety limits.
5
SettingsSwitch theme, export/import your workflows as JSON, or reset to the 15 starter workflows.
