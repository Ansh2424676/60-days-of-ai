# Day 40 – AI Product Design

## Project Name

The Story Desk – AI Data Storytelling Assistant

---

## Overview

The Story Desk is an AI-powered assistant that transforms raw business findings into clear, stakeholder-ready data stories.

Instead of functioning as a generic chatbot, it guides users through a structured conversation, identifies missing business context, evaluates the narrative, and generates professional recommendations with measurable success metrics.

---

## Features

- Multi-turn AI conversation
- Data storytelling coach
- Business narrative generation
- Story grading
- Structure, Clarity & Insight scoring
- Executive recommendations
- Premium responsive UI
- Claude API Integration
